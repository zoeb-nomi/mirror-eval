## Judge validation

Two judges from different model families (`claude`, `gemini`) tagged every answer independently. Exact tag-set agreement: **117/332 (35%)**; on the interpretive categories alone, **35%**. Pre-registered expectation was 60–75%, with below-60% pre-declared to mean the taxonomy is underspecified. It is. That is why this report bands.

Tags per answer: `claude` 1.62 · `gemini` 0.55. A stable ratio here across rubric revisions is a model-level disposition, not a prompt problem, and no further rubric work will move it.
